Support

Need help with ChordSight? Email pallabsonowal999@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

What's free?

C, Am and Em with fingerstyle are free forever, along with chord details, camera controls, tempo, the capo and the two starter chord sheets.

How does the free trial work?

Tap any locked chord or pattern and choose Start 1-day free trial. It costs nothing and needs no payment details. For one day you can use every chord, strumming and your own chord sheets. Afterwards the app goes back to the free set. Sheets you made are kept.

How do I unlock everything?

One purchase unlocks all chords, strumming and creating or editing chord sheets for good. There is no subscription. The unlock works with Family Sharing.

I already paid. How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the guitar to show the controls, tap the gear, choose ChordSight, then Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I see the hands up close?

Pinch towards a hand, or double-tap it, to focus the camera there. Drag to change the angle, and double-tap again to return to the whole guitar.

I can't hear anything.

Check the volume and any connected Bluetooth headphones. ChordSight plays even when the ring/silent switch is set to silent.

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later.
