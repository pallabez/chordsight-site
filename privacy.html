ChordSight

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

ChordSight is a guitar-learning app for iPhone and iPad made by Pawry. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: ChordSight does not collect any data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. ChordSight has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server of ours. We cannot see how you use the app, what you play or anything on your device.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves a few things on your device only:

  • Your settings, such as tempo, capo and whether chord details are shown.
  • The chord sheets you create or edit.
  • A date the app uses to keep the free trial working correctly if the device clock changes.

This information is never sent to us. It may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this information from the device.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple Account, payment details or any other personal information from these purchases. Apple's handling of them is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you, and you can turn sharing off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

ChordSight is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
